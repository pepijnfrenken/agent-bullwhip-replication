Dear Dr Long and colleagues,

My name is Pepijn Frenken. I am an independent researcher in Eindhoven; the TU/e
cluster provided compute only. For the past several months I have run a
pre-registered adaptation study of the post-training claim in v4 of
arXiv:2605.17036, and I am writing before I put it on arXiv, because you are the
only people who can tell me whether I have read your protocol correctly.

Two findings, the first of which is about your paper rather than your method.

Your own rule is never scored. Equation 5 gives the exponentially smoothed
forecast and equation 6 the order-up-to target, and Appendix C.1 carries that
model analytically, but I could not find anywhere in v4 a result in which the
tuned rule is scored as a cost baseline against the LLM arms. So I built it as my
reference: two parameters fitted on weeks 1 to 18 of a 36-week game, then played
frozen. It costs 5,346 in median over 30 demand paths. The closest any arm I
trained gets is 1.75x that, and only at its best seed.

The second is the ladder. At 4B, post-training beats its own untrained baseline:
9,346 against 21,578 at seed 0 (30 of 30 paired paths) and 17,958 at seed 1 (25 of
30, and marginal against a 15% bar). At 1.7B it is adverse on all three seeds,
between 2.4x and 8.2x the untrained cost. At 0.6B it is negative, but that rung
used SFT and RLCD rather than your RL objective, so I do not read it as evidence
about your recipe. At 8B, one of four arms passes, 14,594 against 89,633 (29 of
30), and it is the arm my frozen selection rule did not pick.

Three questions, none of which is a request for approval:

1. Is my reading of equations 5 and 6 right, and is it right that v4 contains no
   tuned-rule comparison? If it does and I missed it, I would rather fix my paper
   than publish the claim that it is absent.

2. Did you see seed variance at 4B? Mine is 1.92x between two seeds of the same
   arm, which makes me suspect that a single-seed result at this scale is more
   fragile than v4 can show.

3. Do per-run artifacts exist behind your reported numbers? Mine are committed and
   public. I would rather compare rows than results.

The paper is attached. The pre-registration, the frozen gates, every per-run file
and a section listing where I deviated from my own protocol are public:
https://github.com/pepijnfrenken/agent-bullwhip-replication
(tag arxiv-submission-1)

I have not asked for your endorsement and I am not asking now. I am asking whether
I am wrong. If I am, I will correct the paper and say where the correction came
from.
